# Structuring Detection Policy

## Purpose

Structuring refers to activity where transactions may be deliberately divided into smaller amounts to avoid detection or reporting thresholds.

## Indicators

Potential structuring indicators include:

- Repeated transactions with similar amounts
- Multiple transactions within a short period
- Transaction amounts clustered below an internal threshold
- Activity involving multiple related accounts
- Repeated deposits or transfers with similar patterns

## Investigation

Investigators should review transaction timing, amounts, related accounts, customer behavior, and historical activity.

Structuring should not be concluded from transaction size alone. Context and supporting evidence are required.
