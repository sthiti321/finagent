# Transaction Velocity Monitoring Policy

## Purpose

Velocity monitoring identifies unusually frequent transaction activity within a defined time period.

## Indicators

Potential risk indicators include:

- Many transactions within a short period
- Sudden increases in transaction frequency
- Rapid incoming and outgoing transfers
- Transaction frequency significantly above customer history

## Review

Velocity should be evaluated relative to the customer's normal behavior.

Investigators should consider transaction amounts, timing, counterparties, and account relationships.

## Escalation

Repeated high-velocity activity combined with other suspicious indicators should receive increased investigation priority.
