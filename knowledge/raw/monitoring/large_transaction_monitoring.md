# Large Transaction Monitoring Policy

## Purpose

Large transaction monitoring identifies transactions whose value is significantly higher than normal customer activity or defined internal thresholds.

## Risk Indicators

A large transaction may require review when:

- The amount exceeds an internal threshold.
- The amount is significantly above the customer's normal transaction size.
- Multiple large transactions occur within a short period.
- Large transactions are connected to unusual account relationships.

## Investigation

Investigators should compare the transaction with:

- Customer history
- Account history
- Previous transaction amounts
- Transaction frequency
- Related accounts
- Source and destination information

Large transaction size alone does not establish suspicious activity.
