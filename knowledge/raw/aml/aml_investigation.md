# AML Investigation Procedure

## Investigation Objective

The objective of an AML investigation is to determine whether observed customer or transaction activity is consistent with expected behavior and whether there are indicators of financial crime.

## Investigation Steps

1. Review the alert details.
2. Identify the customer and account involved.
3. Review recent and historical transactions.
4. Compare activity with the customer's normal behavior.
5. Review transaction amounts, timing, locations, merchants, and devices.
6. Identify related accounts and transactions.
7. Investigate incoming and outgoing fund flows.
8. Look for repeated, circular, or coordinated activity.
9. Document supporting evidence and findings.

## Network Investigation

Investigators should examine relationships between accounts when transactions indicate possible movement of funds through multiple accounts.

Important patterns include:

- Fan-in activity
- Fan-out activity
- Circular fund flows
- Shared devices
- Suspicious transfer paths
- Connected account clusters

## Investigation Outcome

An investigation may result in:

- No concern identified
- Alert closed with explanation
- Monitoring recommended
- Escalation to a higher review level
- Case creation

An alert is not proof of financial crime. Decisions should be based on the available evidence.
