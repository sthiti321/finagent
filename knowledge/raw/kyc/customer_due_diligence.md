# Customer Due Diligence Policy

## Purpose

Customer Due Diligence (CDD) establishes an understanding of the customer and helps identify risks associated with the customer relationship.

## Customer Information

CDD should consider available information such as:

- Customer identity
- Account information
- Customer profile
- Expected account activity
- Geographic information
- Business or employment information
- Source of funds where applicable

## Risk Assessment

Customer risk should consider factors including:

- Customer characteristics
- Geographic risk
- Product or service risk
- Transaction behavior
- Unusual activity
- Historical alerts

## Ongoing Monitoring

Customer information and activity should be reviewed periodically and when significant changes occur.

Unexpected transaction behavior should be compared with the customer's established profile.

## Documentation

CDD reviews should document relevant information, risk factors, decisions, and supporting evidence.
