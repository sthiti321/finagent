# Enhanced Due Diligence Policy

## Purpose

Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) provides additional review for customers or relationships presenting elevated financial crime risk.

## EDD Triggers

EDD may be appropriate when there are:

- High customer risk indicators
- Complex ownership structures
- Unusual transaction activity
- Significant transaction volumes
- High-risk geographic exposure
- Repeated suspicious alerts
- Complex account networks

## Additional Review

EDD may include deeper review of:

- Customer background
- Expected activity
- Source of funds
- Transaction history
- Related accounts
- Connected entities
- Previous investigation results

## Documentation

The investigator should document the reason for EDD, information reviewed, risk factors identified, and final assessment.
