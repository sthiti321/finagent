# Financial Network Investigation Procedure

## Objective

Network investigation identifies relationships between accounts and transactions that may indicate coordinated financial activity.

## Network Indicators

Investigators should consider:

- Shared devices
- Multiple accounts sending funds to one account
- One account sending funds to multiple accounts
- Circular fund flows
- Suspicious transfer paths
- Connected account clusters

## Network Risk

Network risk increases when multiple suspicious relationships occur together.

Investigators should examine the transactions forming the relationship rather than relying only on the existence of a connection.

## Documentation

Investigators should record relevant accounts, transactions, relationships, network patterns, and supporting evidence.
