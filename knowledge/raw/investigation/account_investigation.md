# Account Investigation Procedure

## Objective

Account investigation evaluates whether account activity is consistent with expected customer behavior.

## Review Areas

Investigators should review:

- Account ownership
- Transaction history
- Transaction amounts
- Transaction frequency
- Merchants
- Locations
- Devices
- Incoming transfers
- Outgoing transfers
- Related accounts

## Behavioral Comparison

Current activity should be compared with historical customer behavior.

Significant deviations may increase investigation priority.

## Evidence

Investigators should document relevant transactions, behavioral deviations, network relationships, and conclusions.
