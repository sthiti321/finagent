# Alert Escalation Policy

## Purpose

Alert escalation ensures that higher-risk or complex alerts receive appropriate review.

## Escalation Indicators

An alert may require escalation when:

- Multiple risk indicators are present.
- Suspicious activity is repeated.
- Significant behavioral deviations are identified.
- Complex account relationships are involved.
- Multiple related alerts exist.
- Network activity indicates coordinated behavior.

## Escalation Information

An escalation should include:

- Customer information
- Account information
- Alert details
- Relevant transactions
- Risk indicators
- Behavioral findings
- Network findings
- Investigator reasoning

## Decision

Escalation should be based on evidence and documented reasoning. An alert should not be escalated solely because of a single weak indicator.
