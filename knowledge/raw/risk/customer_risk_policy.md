# Customer Risk Policy

## Purpose

Customer risk assessment determines the relative financial crime risk associated with customer activity.

## Risk Factors

Risk assessment may consider:

- Customer characteristics
- Transaction behavior
- Transaction frequency
- Geographic activity
- Merchant activity
- Device relationships
- Historical alerts
- Network relationships

## Risk Levels

Customers may be categorized as:

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH

Risk classification should consider multiple relevant indicators rather than relying on one factor.

## Review

Customer risk should be reassessed when significant changes in behavior or other risk indicators are observed.
