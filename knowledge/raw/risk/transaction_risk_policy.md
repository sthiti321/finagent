# Transaction Risk Policy

## Purpose

Transaction risk assessment estimates the relative risk associated with individual transactions.

## Risk Factors

Relevant factors may include:

- Transaction amount
- Transaction time
- Transaction frequency
- Customer behavior deviation
- Location
- Merchant
- Device
- Transaction type
- Payment method
- Account relationships

## Risk Levels

Transactions may be classified as:

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH

## Investigation Priority

High-risk transactions should receive higher investigation priority, particularly when multiple independent indicators support the elevated risk.

Risk scores support investigation and should not automatically be interpreted as confirmed financial crime.
